Stars Added ⭐ ​

  • Starred r/CrackWatch in Gaming Tools, popular and useful piracy game release tracking group.

  • Starred HizSearch in Game Download, multi-site game download search engine, uses trusted sources, has a nice UI, and active development.

  • Starred NASA2Explore in Astronomy section, the official source of all NASA images, including full metadata.

  • Starred LocalTube Manager in YouTube Tools, extension that allows you to use YouTubes features without an account, including subscribing, playlists, and notifications, also allows you to export data to sync across devices.

  • Starred OpenCode in AI Coding Agents, has good free models + seems to work well for people.

  • Starred DOS Zone in Browser Emulators, retro PC browser emulator that works well + has a big library.

  • Starred Shizuku Fork in Android Device, fixes multiple features, adds QoL updates, and is maintained well.

  • Starred ⁠SteaMidra in Steam Tools, multi-tool that includes DLC unlockers, multiplayer fixes, and a manifest tool.

  • Starred ReadYou in Android RSS Readers, clean design, offline support, Google Reader + Fever API sync

  • Starred Gadgetbridge in Android Utilities, open-source gadget manager w/ support for 300+ devices and no account required.

  • Starred SmartTube in Android TV, works on lots of TVs, has improved navigation, and is preferred over TizenTube by some.

  • Starred Lucas Chess + Chess Tempo in Educational Chess section. Feature-rich chess learning platforms both of which seem to be well liked and good for beginners.

  • Starred nakarte in Maps section, world map with multiple layers and street views all combined into one.

  • Starred OpenFront in Browser Strategy Games, open-source Conquest / War style game with active multiplayer community.

  • Starred setlist.fm in Concerts / Live section, in-depth database of setlists for almost every concert and tour in history.

  • Starred GiveWell in Charity / Donations section, charity research group, publishes their research process online, and charges no fees.

  • Added Spicy Lyrics next to Spicetify. Improved lyrics with very nice UI, not technically a star, but people seem to really like it so it felt worth mentioning.
